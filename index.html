FEATURED PROJECT

Phys Paint

Layer-based physics for Blender, built around a direct painting workflow instead of repetitive setup.

Portfolio demo · 5:25 Open on YouTube ↗

In development

Paint where physics should happen.

Phys Paint organizes physics into independent layers. Artists can paint influence directly on a character or mesh, tune each layer separately, manage collisions, and preserve a readable workflow while iterating.

  • 01
    Layer-based workflowSeparate physical behaviors without turning the scene into a modifier maze.
  • 02
    Weight-painted influenceUse familiar Blender painting tools to define where the effect applies.
  • 03
    Per-layer controlsAdjust softness, bounce, damping and related behavior independently.
  • 04
    Collision workflowControl how painted areas interact with external colliders.

ABOUT

One brand, more than one tool.

Nexazem is not limited to Blender. It is the umbrella for software tools, 3D utilities and creative experiments built around one idea: make demanding workflows clearer, faster and easier to control.

I’m Nick Anderson Azémar, a 3D creator and tool developer. My current focus includes physics, animation, simulation and practical workflow design for artists.

Build useful tools. Keep the interface understandable. Let creators stay in control.
